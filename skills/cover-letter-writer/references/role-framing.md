# Role Framing

## Junior Roles

Emphasize learning speed, relevant fundamentals, practical projects, reliability, and motivation grounded in the actual role.

Avoid presenting the candidate as overqualified unless the source material clearly supports that framing and the role benefits from it.

## Mid-Level Roles

Emphasize proven execution, relevant tools, collaboration habits, and examples of owning work without inflating scope.

## Senior Roles

Emphasize judgment, autonomy, impact, mentoring, architecture, stakeholder work, or ownership only when backed by source material.

## Specialist and IC Roles

Lead with depth, craft, relevant systems, domain exposure, and execution quality.

Downplay people management, broad leadership, or executive-style framing unless the ad explicitly asks for it.

## Bridging Skill Gaps

When the user lacks an exact requirement, bridge with adjacent evidence:

- Similar tools or domains
- Demonstrated learning speed
- Transferable technical patterns
- Relevant project constraints

Do not claim direct experience when the evidence only supports adjacency.
